Results

Faster. More accurate. More consistent.

Twelve teaching assistants graded real quiz answers, by hand and with VeriGrader, and every mark was checked against an expert-consensus ground truth.

Formative study

141 graded exam papers analysed
5 stakeholder interviews

Controlled user study

12 teaching assistants with grading experience
15 real student answers per question, from in-class quizzes
2 subjects: data structures & numerical methods
